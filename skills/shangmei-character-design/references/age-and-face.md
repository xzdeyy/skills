# Age and Face Guide

Age must be visible in multiple systems, not only hair color.

## 12–16
- softer jaw and unfinished facial maturity
- smoother skin
- adolescent proportions
- do not render as an adult beauty

## 18–25
- young facial volume
- minimal wrinkles
- clearer jaw and quicker posture

## 30–40
- mild eye-corner lines
- slight nasolabial structure
- more mature cheek/jaw definition

## 40–50
- visible but restrained eye bags
- eye-corner lines
- mature nasolabial area
- slightly softer cheeks and jaw
- possible mild hairline recession or gray strands
- older-looking hands and more settled posture

A 45-year-old must not read as 25.

## 55+
- heavier eyelids
- clearer wrinkles and cheek softness
- mixed gray or white hair when appropriate
- slightly forward posture possible
- slower, more settled movement impression

Do not make every older person extremely frail.

## Face-shape pool
Use variety: square, long rectangular, round, oval, heart/seed-like, long face, broad short face, narrow upper/wide lower, high cheekbones, low cheekbones, broad chin, short chin, projecting jaw.

Do not default all women to the same pointed face or all men to the same square face.

## Feature design
Design brows, eyes, nose, mouth, ears and chin separately. Personality should appear through structure and habitual expression rather than a generic “good/evil” face.

Examples:
- calculating shopkeeper: thinner straighter brows, narrower observant eyes, rounded nose tip, thinner compressed lips
- honest farmer: stronger brows, rounder/open eyes, wider nose tip, fuller mouth
- severe elder: tighter eye area, downward mouth corners, judging gaze; do not simply make the person ugly.
