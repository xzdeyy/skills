# Occupation and Wardrobe Guide

Use these as starting logic, not rigid uniforms.

## Farmer
Body/posture: labor-shaped shoulders and hands, practical stance.
Clothing: coarse cotton-linen cross-collar top or short work jacket, loose practical trousers, cloth shoes, cloth belt, optional head cloth/simple tied hair.
Palette: gray blue, earth brown, faded indigo, muted ochre.
Avoid luxurious embroidery, fantasy boots, noble headwear.

## Grain-shop / general shop owner
Body/posture: slightly settled or mildly stocky build is plausible, composed counter-side posture, observant/calculating gesture.
Clothing: clean plain long shirt or robe, darker short outer jacket optional, ordinary cloth belt, black cloth shoes, traditional merchant/shopkeeper cloth cap when requested.
Palette: dark brown, gray brown, deep indigo, smoke gray.
Signal wealth through neatness and fabric quality, not ornament overload.

## Scholar
Body/posture: relatively slender when appropriate, controlled posture.
Clothing: plain scholar robe/cross-collar garment, simple cloth headwear or restrained cap, cloth shoes.
Palette: gray blue, rice white, muted cyan, light brown.
Avoid immortal robes, glowing ornaments, excessive floating ribbons.

## Constable / yamen runner
Body/posture: functional, alert, stronger stance than a scholar.
Clothing: practical official service clothing appropriate to the story period, dark or muted blue-gray palette, simple belt and functional footwear, badge/waist token only if needed.
Avoid fantasy armor.

## Wealthy merchant
Body/posture: composed, stable, occupies space confidently.
Clothing: better woven cloth or restrained silk, cleaner tailoring, modest quality accessories.
Palette: deeper tea brown, dark green, muted maroon, indigo.
Avoid covering the character in gold.

## Ordinary married woman
Hair: low bun/arranged hair, wood pin or simple cloth accessory.
Clothing: practical cross-collar top, skirt or trousers depending setting, cotton-linen/plain cotton.
Palette: muted blue, brown, bean green, old red, rice white.

## Girl / young adolescent
Hair: age-appropriate buns, tied hair, simple cloth ribbon.
Clothing: modest and practical. Youth should read through face and proportions, not merely brighter colors.

## Elderly woman
Hair: low bun, mixed gray hair, wood pin.
Clothing: layered practical cloth, subdued palette.
Age must be in face and posture, not just gray hair.

## Innkeeper / tavern owner
Clothing: practical clean layers; apron only when useful; sleeves easy to work in.
Gesture: accustomed to greeting, counting, pouring and observing customers.

## Gambler / idler
Do not make “bad character” equal dirty monster. Use slightly careless grooming, loosened clothing discipline, restless hands/eyes, worn edges, perhaps one aspirational accessory above his means.
